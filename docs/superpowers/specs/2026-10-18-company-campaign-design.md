# Company discovery and generation campaign

Target 50 distinct companies and five accepted questions each (250 total), using
the existing open-web validator. Company research remains the example domain.
The company is the reference identity: each question identifies it through different
research requirements. X stays self-contained; company allocation, Y, E and all
discovery metadata stay private. Historical configurations and runs stay intact.

Use one journal, one aggregate budget and one source store per campaign, with
separate evidence graphs per company. Do not create 50 independently budgeted runs.
Discovery proposes companies across industries, regions and funding stages through
observed Codex search. Proposals are not qualified companies. Only observed URLs
may enter discovery evidence; snapshots, independent identity/classification checks
and supported connected facts establish qualification. Collect funding, founders,
products, acquisitions, partnerships and investor relationships where evidenced;
absence is never fabricated. A company need not have undergone an acquisition.

Qualification requires at least three primary dated sources, three relationship
families, five connected entities, and five possible family combinations. These
are readiness screens, not proof that five questions will validate. Defaults seek
at least ten industries, five regions and four funding-stage categories. Report
actual supported coverage and any shortfall rather than filling missing categories.

Schedule companies in a balanced order, give each a first question opportunity,
then revisit them for remaining slots. Default minimum accepted depths are
0, 1, 1, 2, 2. Each slot permits bounded attempts/revisions. A retained parent
cannot satisfy a deeper slot. Count one final question per seed lineage. Require
the same canonical company reference throughout expansion. Reject equivalent
queries, paraphrases, and near-identical research requirements before quota credit;
independently judge borderline semantic diversity. A different date or more words
does not make a different research requirement.

All existing construction, closed-book, actual browsing, citation, temporal,
alternative and whole/partial shortcut gates remain mandatory. Question-only
solver inputs and filesystem isolation remain unchanged. Add typed partnership
events so parties, products and dates cannot join unrelated partnerships.

Default campaign allowance stays 1,447,740 tokens, 49 calls, 13 web actions,
concurrency one and $0 additional API/search spend. This allowance cannot promise
250 accepted questions. Preserve progress and pause; no automatic budget increase.
An explicit, idempotent budget-extension file can authorize additional local
allowance on resume without changing frozen acceptance rules. Unknown remote calls
remain blocked, and subscription retries still require the existing explicit flag.

Export private discovery, qualification, allocation and diversity records, plus
coverage and per-company accepted counts, depth distributions, browsing attempts,
failure reasons, shortfalls and attributed usage. Public export stays X only.
Generated, structurally valid, validated and quota-credited counts remain distinct.
