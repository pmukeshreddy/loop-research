## S2 field gaps and quotation repair

During B0-1's review, the reviewer asked whether “missing-required-field gap” meant only an entirely empty cell. The parent reads the registered gap criterion to include an explicitly observed missing required value **or a supported complementary component** (for example, a missing concurrent affiliation), even when the cell already contains another component. Pure quotation-format or quotation-context repair for an otherwise supported value is excluded. A successful fetch must be explicitly directed at a gap visible in executed code/output. Discovery of a lead and a successful download do not by themselves establish that the source closes the gap.

This boundary was sent to all three reviewers before their final B manual scores. The B0-1 reviewer retains all 81 successful post-gap new URLs with inclusion/exclusion reasons. Its initial 26 empty nationality fields led to 74 successful unique URLs. Later Kajita/ICRR and LIGO affiliation omissions are missing components. The additional Thouless Commons fetch is excluded: earlier retained evidence already supported British and American nationality, and the later action sought a clearer contiguous quotation without adding a missing value component. The provisional S2 count is therefore 76; the final signed review, rather than this interim discussion, is authoritative.
