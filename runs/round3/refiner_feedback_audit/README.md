# Refinement output representation audit

The failed awake preflight printed 2,981,867 characters of complete trajectory output and then dispatched a 3,039,692-character prompt. The product correction uses host-owned complete output records and conditional whole-iteration references. This dependent audit change makes that new representation reviewable; it does not alter research or refinement execution.

`verify_refinement_context` rejects unknown or inconsistent output policies and checks the declared complete initial conversation before saved-replay process dispatch. For archives declaring `whole-iteration-reference-v1`, the transport audit rebuilds the planner conversation from its original instructions/limits, actual successful planner responses, exact executed code and every completed stdout/stderr block. It deterministically recomputes each representation change, requires its complete event before the affected dispatch, and compares every actual planner prompt byte for byte. Reads must refer to a previously completed iteration and match the original stream/block hashes. Complete streams, code, execution time, final status and historical prompts remain in their original archives. The audit writes no source archive.

The audit rejects altered streams, injected code, internally self-consistent but forged receipts, altered read hashes, reads before iteration completion, missing/late/duplicate references, and changed initial messages. It preserves the old archive contract when the new policy is absent; an output reference event without its policy is rejected. Ordinary research root/sub attribution remains separate from refinement planner/sub calls.

This is a disclosed refinement-feedback representation change, not byte-identical refinement prompts. Full-data accessibility is not evidence that the model semantically inspected every occurrence. Research-root prompts and measurements, the supplied logical snapshot, candidate accounting, strict proposal schema, refinement timing, models and budgets are unchanged. No prefix/suffix clipping or output summaries are added.

Verification: the unchanged baseline had 21 passing audit tests. Three policy and one history regression tests failed before their audit implementation. The expanded real-worker audit suite passed 36 tests in 9.68 seconds; the broader audit/replay/renderer/context suite passed 113 in 20.95 seconds. The real isolated worker prints over 1 MB, restores its exact stdout and stderr through the host, produces a small later observation and submits a strict valid proposal; ten independent mutations of that archive are rejected. Existing tests were retained. Raw RED/GREEN logs and exact source hashes are adjacent. The copied product prerequisites are owned by the separate transport commit and are not part of this audit commit.
